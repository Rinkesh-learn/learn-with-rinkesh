Meet Rinkesh.

Learn With Rinkesh teaches Excel the way it's actually used at work — real formulas, real corporate scenarios, real practice. Not another course that stops at theory.

Why this exists

Most Excel content either stays too basic to matter at work, or jumps into advanced tricks nobody explains properly. This platform was built to close that gap — real formulas, real corporate scenarios, and an actual spreadsheet to practice in, not a multiple-choice quiz. The goal is simple: get comfortable enough to open a blank sheet at work and actually build something.

About the Developer

Rinkesh is a Business Analyst with 5+ years in the data industry — a journey that's moved from US logistics, into healthcare revenue cycle management, and now into legal and business process automation. Excel has been the constant thread throughout, alongside hands-on experience with SQL, Power Apps, and Power Automation. This platform brings that same data-industry perspective to how Excel is taught.
