Hi, I'm Rinkesh.

I built Learn With Rinkesh to teach Excel the way it's actually used at work — real formulas, real corporate scenarios, real practice. Not another course that stops at theory.

Why this exists

Most Excel content either stays too basic to be useful on the job, or jumps straight into advanced tricks nobody explains properly. This platform is built to close that gap — a formula reference with real corporate use cases, a genuine spreadsheet to practice in (not a multiple-choice quiz), and questions that actually get harder as you improve. The goal isn't to help you memorize functions. It's to get you comfortable enough to open a blank sheet at work and actually build something.

About me

I'm a Business Analyst working in legal and business process automation, with 5+ years of experience in the data industry. My path wasn't a straight line — I've worked across a few very different industries, and Excel has been the one constant skill that traveled with me through every one of them.
